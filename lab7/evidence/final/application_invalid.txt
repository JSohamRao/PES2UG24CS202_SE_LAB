==== Running parse_seat ====
Enter a seat number (e.g. H12): Invalid seat; use a row A-Z and a positive number.

==== Running total_price ====
Enter ticket prices as CSV (e.g. 150,250,400): Invalid prices; use nonnegative integers separated by commas.

==== Running average_rating ====
Enter ratings separated by spaces (e.g. 4 5 3): Invalid ratings; use integers from 1 to 5 separated by spaces.
